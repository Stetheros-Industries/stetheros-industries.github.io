Goods and services from Stetheros Industries

A member-run industrial company headquartered in Stetheros, Prunia. Our members produce raw materials, processed goods, and services, which the company sells through its shops to customers of every nation.

Shop prices govern

Prices on this page are for reference. The price charged in the company's shops is the price that applies, because shop prices account for discounts, taxes, and currency exchange that this page cannot.

The official catalog

The goods listed here are the company's official catalog under §3.2 of the policy document, and the services are its service schedule under §8.4.

See your price with a discount

Citizens and governments of nations under the Home Charter or the Economic Plan receive a discount on company goods (§11.4). This is an estimate: the shop applies the actual discount at the time of sale.

All prices are in Prunian Credits (Cr). The Credit is the currency of Prunia, where the company is headquartered, and the only currency the company uses for prices, payments, and banking (§1.4).

Custom and bulk orders

Need something that isn't on the shelf, or a larger quantity than a shop holds? Place a custom order with the company. When it is ready, you can collect it at any of our stores, or have it delivered at a rate quoted by a board member.

Goods with a bulk price are discounted when you order at least the quantity shown on the item. Custom orders are quoted by the company's Sales role, and payment is held in escrow until the order is complete (§8.3).
