About Stetheros Industries

Scale. Strength. Stetheros.

Stetheros Industries was founded in 2024 by FighterPilot O5 in the city of Stetheros, Prunia. The company takes its name from its home city, where it is still headquartered. From there the company has grown into an industrial producer that trades across borders, selling to customers and working with governments in every nation that will deal fairly with it.

The company is run by its members. They mine, refine, manufacture, and build; the company brings their work to market through its shops. Pooling production lets every member sell at a scale none could reach alone, and each member's reward follows what that member actually contributes.

Founded in Stetheros, 2024

Our headquarters remain in Stetheros, our core infrastructure and storage are in Prunia, and all company banking is in the Prunian Credit. Prunia's government and citizens hold the company's Home Charter, the agreement that ties the company to its home nation.

Trading internationally

Our shops and customers span many nations. Any nation can sign the company's Economic Plan, which gives its citizens the same 10% discount Prunia's receive and a share of the company's profit pool. In wartime the company takes no side: civilian trade continues to every partner nation.

What we stand for

We honor our deals

An agreement with a customer, a member, or a nation is kept as written. Payments due are paid on time, and a late reimbursement carries a premium the company pays, not the member.

Quality you can build on

Every good is recorded as it enters company storage and sold under the company's name, so the company stands behind what it sells. Services are paid into escrow and released only when the job is done.

Fair to the people who make it

Members keep 80% of what their goods earn, share in the company's profit, and can see the ledger, the payments, and the approvals.

Steady through hard times

Trade continues in war and in peace. Partner nations stay supplied, and goods important in a war never reach the enemy of the nation that made them.

Our mark

The five diamonds in our logo trace the path every product takes, from the ground to the customer. Together they form a hidden arrow pointing right: the production chain moving forward.

  1. Raw materialsOre, timber, crops, and stone: what our members gather from the land.
  2. RefiningSmelting, pressing, and processing raw materials into usable stock.
  3. Manufactured productsComponents, machines, and finished goods made from refined stock.
  4. ServicesDelivery, construction, and the work that puts products to use.
  5. The consumerThe customer at the end of the chain, and the reason for every step before it.
